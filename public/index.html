Slip ratio vs. ABS target band
Pedal press → hydraulic pressure reaches this wheel's valve → piston pushes the pad onto the spinning rotor → torque slows the wheel. If slip climbs past target, the ABS ECU commands this valve to hold or release pressure so the pad backs off before the wheel locks.
